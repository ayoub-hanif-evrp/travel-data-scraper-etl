## Source attribution

Sample travel data in `data/sample/` (and any local outputs under `data/processed/` or `data/raw/` generated by the demo) is extracted from publicly accessible Wikivoyage destination articles.

Each record includes a `source_url` field pointing back to the originating Wikivoyage page for provenance.

## What this project redistributes

This demonstration prefers short structured listing facts (name, category, address, phone, coordinates when present, concise descriptions). It does not copy Wikivoyage images and avoids redistributing large narrative article passages.
